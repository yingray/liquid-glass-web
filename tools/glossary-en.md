## Capitalization rule (follows Apple)
- Title Case: buttons, menu items, tab labels, segmented items, section and group headers, sheet titles, toolbar labels.
- Sentence case: descriptions, hints, captions, messages, toasts, callout text, placeholders.
- Never all caps.
